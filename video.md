## 💡 Pro-Tips for Recording Your YouTube Video

1. **Resolution & Scaling**:
   - Set your screen resolution to **1920x1080 (1080p)** with browser zoom at **100% or 90%** for maximum crispness.
2. **Lighting for Webcam Demo**:
   - Ensure your face is evenly lit when demonstrating live face recognition so YuNet and SFace achieve $>95\%$ confidence.
3. **Cursor Visibility**:
   - Use a clear mouse pointer (or enable cursor highlight in OBS/Screen Recorder) so viewers can follow your clicks.
4. **Audio Quality**:
   - Speak with energetic, clear pacing. The entire script above takes exactly **3 minutes 35 seconds** at normal speaking speed.
